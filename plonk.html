Case study

Plonk!

From brand identity to launch ads: a playful visual world built to sell absurd products with serious stopping power.

Branding

Plonk! is a novelty-first brand, so the identity needs to feel loud, fast, and a little ridiculous. We built a visual system that looks intentionally over-the-top while still reading clearly at mobile sizes.

The logo anchors every execution, while high-contrast product visuals and tight copy create a consistent tone from post to post.

Campaign ads

Each ad follows the same structure: one absurd product, one instantly readable punchline, and one clear visual payoff. Together they feel like a system, not one-offs.
