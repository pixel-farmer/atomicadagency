Case study

Plonk!

From brand identity to launch ads: a playful visual world built to sell absurd products with serious stopping power.

Branding

Plonk! is a product brand developed within a virtual world, where the identity leans playful but remains sharply defined.

The visual language blends nostalgia with a more contemporary edge—using familiarity to draw people in, then pushing it just far enough to feel unexpected and memorable.

What we did

  • Brand concept and positioning
  • Product design
  • Naming and narrative development
  • Campaign concepts and ad creative
  • 3D modeling, rendering, and visual direction
  • In-world merchandising and presentation

    • Why is matters

    Building a product brand inside a virtual world introduces constraints similar to real-world launches—limited attention, immediate competition, and the need to create clarity and intrigue within seconds.

    Plonk became a way to prototype campaign ideas, test visual storytelling approaches, and develop brand systems in a fast, low-friction environment.

    The result is simple: stronger ideas, better visibility, and a clear increase in sales once campaign-based creative was introduced and shared with a targeted in-world audience.

    Campaign ads

    Each ad follows the same structure: one absurd product, one instantly readable punchline, and one clear visual payoff. Together they feel like a system, not one-offs.
