Case study

Plonk!

Print and social creative that exposes what X-ray glasses were never supposed to reveal.

The idea

Plonk! sells novelty with a wink. The brief was simple: stop thumbs mid-scroll and make the joke land in under three seconds—then reward anyone who looked twice.

We leaned into retro comic-book absurdity and a headline that does the heavy lifting, so the visual punchline feels inevitable, not random.

Execution

Key art was built for OOH and feed placements, with type and color tuned to read clean on a phone and bold at bus-shelter scale. Copy stays tight; the product is the gag.
